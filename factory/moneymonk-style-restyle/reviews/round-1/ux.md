VERDICT: REWORK
## Summary
Ran the app on a scratch DB (port 8791) and used it with seeded boekingen. Mostly good: drawer (opens via htmx, X/Escape close, focus returns to the trigger, scroll locked), upload modal (focus on close button, dropzone, plain-Dutch help), grootboek modal re-opens with a plain-Dutch error ("Rekeningnummer 1100 bestaat al. Kies een ander nummer." with the form values kept), unbalanced boeking error ("Debet en credit zijn niet gelijk: er ontbreekt EUR 5,00 aan de creditkant. Pas een bedrag aan..."), missing boekjaar error with a clear next step, sidebar collapse (persists in localStorage, aria-label toggles, still 10 links with title), settings nav ("Boekhouding" + 3 links with sub-lines and info icons), /help (plain Dutch first, term in parentheses, searchable), help FAB present, inbox stat cards, 404 for unknown ids. Glossary/plain-language rule looks respected: no bare terms, info markers on grootboek/dagboek/boekjaar/debet/credit/btw in nav, drawer, modal.
One acceptance criterion is broken (horizontal page scroll at 375px).

## Blocking findings
- [medium] AC13 (no horizontal page scroll at 375px): measured in a 375px-wide iframe, `documentElement.scrollWidth` exceeds the viewport and the page really scrolls sideways (`scrollTo(500,0)` gives scrollX): /boeken 623 vs 371, /instellingen/grootboek 511, /instellingen/dagboeken 497, /instellingen/boekjaren 501, /boeken/nieuw 445. /inbox, /relaties, /help, /bank are fine. On /boeken, hiding `.table-wrap` removes the overflow, so the table wrapper (`app.css:411`, inside `.card--flush` with `overflow: clip`) leaks width to the page; `contain: paint` on the card also fixed it in my probe. On settings pages and the new-boeking form, the absolutely positioned `.info-tip` tooltips and the `.settings-nav__group` strip (right edge 419 at 375px) extend past the viewport. Done = at 375px none of these pages scroll sideways (the table and nav strip scroll inside their own container, tooltips are clamped or do not contribute to layout width).

## Nice-to-have
- Drawer boeking lines table is 979px wide in a 437px wrapper, so "Bedrag rechts (Credit)", Btw-tarief and Toelichting need sideways scrolling and the credit amount is out of sight on first open. Consider shorter headers or fewer columns in the drawer variant (e.g. drop the bookkeeping term from the th and keep it in the tooltip, or hide Toelichting).
- No-JS fallback: the modal renders as an in-page card (good), but the "Annuleren" button (type=button, data-modal-close) does nothing without JS; hide it under `html:not(.js)` or make it a link back.
- Inbox warning "Uitlezen kan nog niet..." mentions "Terminal", `claude auth login` and `.env` (pre-existing, jargon for a non-technical user).
- Boekjaren/dagboeken pages were not visually checked in dark/light beyond markup; I only exercised dark mode screenshots.
