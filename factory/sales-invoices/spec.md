# Spec: sales-invoices (verkoopfacturen, debiteuren, creditnota's, herinneringen)

## 1. Goal and context
The owner creates a verkoopfactuur for a klant as a concept, edits it freely, then makes it definitief. At that moment, in one transaction, the system assigns the next gapless invoice number, posts a balanced entry in the verkoopdagboek (VK), freezes all invoice data (including a snapshot of own and customer details) and stores a PDF. A finalized invoice can never be edited; corrections are done with a creditnota that references the original. The screen `/verkoop` (now a placeholder) becomes the sales overview with an invoice list and a debiteuren overview with ageing ("ouderdomsanalyse"). Bank reconciliation learns to match incoming payments to open sales invoices. Overdue invoices get a betalingsherinnering (PDF + copyable text).

Builds on (existing code, read it first):
- `app/domain/company/` (`require_company_for_invoice`, `CompanyProfile.missing_for_invoice()`; fields name/address/kvk/btw_id/iban), `app/models/company.py`.
- `app/models/relations.py` `Contact` (`is_customer`, address, `btw_number`, `kvk`, `email`), `app/domain/relations/`.
- Ledger: `app/domain/ledger/entries.py` (`NewEntry/NewLine/validate_entry`), `ledger.service.post_entry/reverse_entry`, periods/locking. Seeded: journal `VK` (type `verkoop`), `1300 Debiteuren`, `3100 BTW af te dragen hoog (21%)`, `3110 BTW af te dragen laag (9%)`, revenue `8000` (default vat NL_21) / `8010` (NL_9) / `8020` (NL_0), vat codes `NL_21/NL_9/NL_0` with `VatCode.sales_rubriek` (1a / 1b / 1e).
- Purchase side as pattern: `app/domain/purchase/{booking,service}.py` (pure `build_entry`, `_open_transaction`, `_system_accounts`, `open_amount`, `invoice_status`, derived storno state via `JournalEntry.reverses_entry_id`), `app/models/purchase.py`, `app/web/purchase_routes.py`, `templates/purchase/`.
- Reconciliation: `app/domain/reconcile/` (`service.PROVIDERS`, `list_open_items`, `OpenItem.side`, `book`, `undo`, `_recompute_paid`, `entries.build_payment_entry`), `app/models/reconcile.py` (`bank_match.source` CHECK, append-only triggers), migration `0011_bank_reconciliation.py`. Its spec explicitly designed `PROVIDERS` so a sales provider can be appended.
- Glossary `app/domain/glossary.py`, `tests/test_plain_language.py`, nav item "Verkoop" (`/verkoop`, term `debiteuren`) in `app/web/navigation.py`.

## 2. Non-goals
- Sending email (decision: no SMTP exists in the project and email intake is a separate backlog item; see section 8). Instead: PDF download + a "Mail aan klant" `mailto:` link with prefilled subject/body (user attaches the downloaded PDF) and a copyable text.
- Verlegde BTW, EU (ICP), export, KOR invoices (codes `VERLEGD`, `EU_*`, `EXPORT`, `GEEN`): refused with a plain message; comes with the BTW-aangifte feature. Only `NL_21`, `NL_9`, `NL_0` are allowed on sales lines.
- Foreign currency, discounts/kortingsregels (use a price), voorschotfacturen/deelfacturen, recurring invoices, quotes (offertes), duplicate/copy-previous-invoice, invoice templates/logo upload, online payment links, interest and incassokosten on reminders.
- Editing or deleting a finalized invoice (never). Deleting a concept is allowed (no number was consumed).
- Voorschot/on-account customer payments (same decision as bank-reconciliation: allocation must be exact), and direct-book of "bij" with BTW (still deferred, but incoming money for a sales invoice is now handled through allocation).
- BTW-aangifte report itself (only the VAT codes/rubriek links on the lines it will read).

## 3. User stories
- As owner I pick a klant, add lines (omschrijving, aantal, prijs excl. BTW, BTW-tarief), see live totals, and make the invoice definitief with one click after a plain-Dutch check ("Dit kun je daarna niet meer wijzigen").
- As owner I download a professional PDF that has everything the Belastingdienst requires, and mail it myself.
- As owner I correct a mistake with a creditnota in two clicks (prefilled from the original).
- As owner I see who still owes me money, how late they are (ouderdom), and send a herinnering.
- As owner I see an incoming bank payment suggested against the right open invoice and confirm it with one click.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

### Numbering and statuses
1. Statuses: `concept` and `definitief`. A concept has no number. Number format `YYYY-NNNN` (e.g. `2026-0001`), YYYY = year of `invoice_date`, NNNN = 4+ digit zero padded counter that restarts at 1 each year. Invoices and creditnota's share one series (still gapless and unique, kind is shown on the document).
2. The number is assigned only inside the finalize transaction, from a counter table (`sales_number_counter`: year PK, last_number), locked with the same `_open_transaction` (BEGIN IMMEDIATE) pattern as purchase. If anything fails (locked period, validation, DB error) the whole transaction rolls back: the counter is not advanced and no number is lost. Test: forced failure after counter increment leaves counter and next number unchanged.
3. Chronological guard: finalize is refused when `invoice_date` is earlier than the `invoice_date` of the most recent finalized document of the same year: "Er is al een factuur met datum {d}. Een factuur mag geen eerdere datum hebben dan de vorige, anders klopt de nummering niet. Kies {d} of later." (Gapless AND ascending dates.)
4. A finalized invoice is immutable: DB triggers abort UPDATE/DELETE on `sales_invoice` rows with status `definitief` (except `paid_cents` and `last_reminder_level`, see below) and any INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on their lines and VAT rows. The service layer also refuses with a plain message: "Deze factuur is definitief en kan niet meer worden gewijzigd. Maak een creditnota om iets te corrigeren."
5. Numbers are never reused. The ledger storno of a sales entry (from the Boeken screen) stays possible; the invoice is then derived as "Gestorneerd" (excluded from debiteuren totals and ageing, number still used). The invoice detail warns: "Corrigeren doe je met een creditnota, niet met een stornoboeking."

### Content (art. 35a Wet OB) and validation at finalize
6. Finalize requires, with a plain Dutch error naming the missing item and where to fix it (link): own `legal_name` (or trade name), own address (line, postcode, plaats), own `kvk`, own `btw_id` (via `require_company_for_invoice`; builder verifies `missing_for_invoice()` covers exactly these and extends it if not), customer name + address (line, postcode, plaats; message "Vul het adres van {klant} in bij Relaties. Het moet op de factuur staan."), `invoice_date`, `due_date` (>= invoice_date), at least one line, every line has omschrijving, aantal > 0, price >= 0, btw-code in {NL_21, NL_9, NL_0}, revenue account (type revenue, active, postable), total incl > 0 (an invoice for 0 euro is refused: "Het totaal is €0,00; zo'n factuur kun je niet boeken.").
7. Optional fields: `service_date` (leverdatum / datum van levering, shown on the PDF when set and different from invoice date, label "Leverdatum"), customer reference ("Uw referentie"), free note (max 500 chars). The customer's BTW-nummer and KvK are printed when known.
8. The PDF/invoice shows: document title (Factuur / Creditnota), unique number, invoice date, leverdatum (if set), vervaldatum + betaaltermijn in days, own name/address/KvK/BTW-id/IBAN/email, customer name/address/BTW-nummer, per line omschrijving, aantal (+ eenheid), prijs per eenheid excl BTW, BTW-tarief %, regelbedrag excl BTW, then totals: totaal excl BTW, per BTW-tarief the grondslag and the BTW-bedrag ("BTW 21% over €100,00: €21,00"), totaal incl BTW, and the payment instruction "Wij ontvangen het bedrag graag vóór {vervaldatum} op {IBAN} onder vermelding van {nummer}". Credit notes additionally state "Creditnota bij factuur {origineel nummer} van {datum}" and show amounts as positive numbers with the title making the direction clear (the PDF header says "Creditnota - u ontvangt dit bedrag terug / dit bedrag wordt verrekend").
9. A finalized document stores a snapshot of seller and buyer fields (see data model) so later changes to Bedrijfsgegevens or the relation never alter an issued invoice or its PDF.

### Amounts and VAT (integer cents; tests first)
10. Line amount: `line_excl = round_half_up(quantity_milli * unit_price_cents / 1000)` (quantity has up to 3 decimals, stored as thousandths). Per BTW-tarief group: `base = sum(line_excl)`; `vat = round_half_up(base * rate_bp / 10000)` computed once per rate group on the sum (never per line, never floats; use `Decimal` or integer arithmetic). `total_excl = sum(base)`, `total_vat = sum(vat)`, `total_incl = total_excl + total_vat`. Stored per rate in `sales_invoice_vat`. The PDF prints exactly the stored numbers.
11. Money is shown in Dutch format (existing helper in `app/domain/money.py`); inputs accept `12,50` and `12.50`.

### Journal entry (on finalize, kind `invoice`)
12. One entry in the first active journal of type `verkoop` (error "Er is geen verkoopdagboek actief. Maak er een aan onder Instellingen." if none): DEBIT `1300 Debiteuren` for `total_incl`; CREDIT per invoice line its revenue account for `line_excl` carrying the line's `vat_code` (grondslag; lines with 0 amount are merged away or refused per ledger rule: lines whose line_excl is 0 are skipped in the entry but remain on the invoice, and if all are 0 the invoice is refused per AC 6); CREDIT per rate group with vat > 0 on `3100` (21%) or `3110` (9%) for `vat`, with NO vat_code on the BTW line (same convention as purchase: only grondslag lines carry the code, so the later BTW-aangifte sums the grondslag per code into rubriek 1a/1b/1e from `VatCode.sales_rubriek`). `booking_date = invoice_date`, `reference = number`, `description = "<klantnaam> - <nummer>"` (max 255), `source_type='verkoopfactuur'`, `source_id` = invoice id; `sales_invoice.journal_entry_id` set in the same transaction. Locked/missing period surfaces the existing plain ledger message; nothing persisted.
13. Credit note (`kind='credit_note'`): same lines with debit/credit swapped (DEBIT revenue and BTW accounts, CREDIT `1300`); amounts positive.
14. The trial balance for `1300` equals the sum of open (non-storno'd) sales invoices minus credit notes plus debiteur opening items (test), and `3100/3110` equal the sum of VAT of non-storno'd documents.

### Credit notes
15. "Creditnota maken" is offered on a finalized invoice (not on a credit note, not on a storno'd invoice). It creates a concept credit note with `credited_invoice_id` = original, prefilled with the original lines (same account, btw-code, price, remaining creditable quantity). The user may lower quantities or remove lines; cannot add lines, change prices or btw-codes (plain message: "Een creditnota corrigeert alleen de oorspronkelijke factuur. Wil je iets anders aanrekenen, maak dan een nieuwe factuur."). Total credited over all finalized credit notes of one invoice must never exceed the original (per line: quantity credited <= quantity invoiced; error "Je kunt maximaal {n} van deze regel crediteren; er is al {m} gecrediteerd.").
16. Credit note finalization uses the same numbering/validation/PDF/entry rules and `invoice_date` guard (AC 3). Its `due_date` equals the invoice date (not payable by the customer).
17. Settlement ("verrekening"): at finalize of a credit note, `applied = min(credit_total, original_open)` is recorded in `sales_credit_application(credit_note_id, invoice_id, amount_cents)` (append-only). No extra journal entry is needed (the credit note entry already reduces 1300). Open amount of the original = `total - paid_cents - sum(applications)`. Remaining credit (`credit_total - applied`) is what you still owe back to the customer: shown as open credit item on the credit note (open = `credit_total - applied - refunded_cents`, where `refunded_cents` = `paid_cents` of the credit note via bank "af" matches).
18. Invoice detail shows linked credit notes and vice versa; invoice status "Gecrediteerd" when open becomes 0 solely through applications, "Betaald" when 0 with payments.

### Payment terms and due dates
19. Betaaltermijn in days: stored on the invoice (`payment_term_days`) and `due_date = invoice_date + days`. Default: the klant's `Contact.payment_term_days` (new nullable column, editable on the relation form for klanten) else 30. Changing date or term in the concept recalculates the due date unless the user typed a due date manually. Term `0` is labelled "Direct betalen".
20. Concept shows the suggested defaults pre-filled and explained: invoice date = today, term, due date, revenue account + btw-code per line (revenue account whose `default_vat_code` matches the line's btw-code; changing btw-code re-suggests the account unless the user chose one), eenheid "stuk".

### Debiteuren and overview
21. `/verkoop` (replaces placeholder): stat cards (Totaal openstaand, Waarvan te laat, Aantal open facturen, Concepten) and tabs "Facturen" (all documents; filter status: Alle / Concept / Openstaand / Te laat / Betaald; search on klant/nummer; pagination as `/inkoop`) and "Debiteuren" (per klant). Row for finalized: number, datum, klant, totaal, openstaand, vervaldatum with "te laat" pill, status chip, PDF link. Rows for concept: "Concept" chip, edit link, no number. Empty state in plain Dutch with link "Maak je eerste factuur".
22. Tab "Debiteuren": per klant open amount (invoices minus credit notes minus applied, plus debiteur opening items) and ageing buckets by days past due on today's date: "Nog niet vervallen", "1-30 dagen", "31-60 dagen", "61-90 dagen", "Meer dan 90 dagen", plus totals row. Click a klant to see its open invoices. Ageing is pure and testable with an injected `today`.
23. Status is derived at read time (no sync hooks): `concept`, `openstaand` (open > 0, not past due), `te laat` (open > 0 and due_date < today), `deels betaald` (0 < paid < total), `betaald`/`gecrediteerd` (open = 0), `gestorneerd` (a reversing entry exists).
24. Opening items of side `debiteur` (beginbalans) are part of the debiteuren overview and ageing, read-only there (as they are shown for crediteuren).

### Bank reconciliation integration
25. A sales provider is appended to `reconcile.service.PROVIDERS` and `list_open_items(side='debiteur')` returns open sales invoices (non-storno'd, open > 0) and credit notes with remaining credit, plus debiteur opening items. `OpenItem.source` gains `'sales_invoice'`; `bank_match.source` CHECK is extended to include it (SQLite: table rebuild in migration 0013 that recreates both append-only triggers verbatim; downgrade symmetrical, refusing if sales matches exist is acceptable).
26. Direction: a transaction `bij` can be matched to sales invoices (and opening debiteur items); a transaction `af` can be matched to sales credit notes (refund to customer). The existing crediteur rules (`af` pays invoices, `bij` matches inkoop-creditnota's) keep working. A transaction allocates to one side only; mixing sides gives a plain error: "Je kunt een betaling niet tegelijk aan verkoop- en inkoopfacturen koppelen. Koppel ze apart."
27. Posting: `bij` paying invoices: DEBIT bank, CREDIT `1300` per allocation; credit note refund (`af`): CREDIT bank, DEBIT `1300`. Entry is in the bank's own dagboek as today. Direct-book lines in the same transaction follow existing rules (bij with btw stays refused).
28. Suggestion signals reuse `matching.suggest` unchanged in logic: customer IBAN equals `counterparty_iban`, open amount equals transaction amount, normalized invoice number (`2026-0007` and `20260007` both match) in description, customer name vs counterparty name. "Zeker" = exact amount AND (number in description OR IBAN match) and exactly one candidate. Multi-invoice suggestion when a description names several invoices of one klant whose open amounts sum to the amount.
29. Allocation updates `sales_invoice.paid_cents` (bank payments only, recomputed from non-reversed `bank_match` rows on undo, as for purchase, never subtract blindly). Over-allocation is refused with the existing message pattern. Undo (storno of the bank entry) restores the open amount.
30. `/verkoop` and the debiteuren overview reflect payments automatically (open amount, status `betaald`), and the reconcile screen shows "Verkoopfactuur 2026-0007 - Klant BV" in the suggestion label. Manual match tab "Past bij factuur" lists the klant's open sales invoices for `bij` transactions, with a klant search field.

### Reminders (herinneringen)
31. A herinnering can be created for a finalized invoice with open > 0 and `due_date < today` (otherwise the button is hidden; direct URL gives a plain error "Deze factuur is nog niet te laat." / "Deze factuur is al betaald."). Levels: 1 "Betalingsherinnering", 2 "Tweede herinnering", 3 "Laatste aanmaning"; the next level is suggested automatically (max 3, further ones repeat level 3 text). Each created reminder is a row in `sales_reminder` (append-only: invoice_id, level, reminder_date (default today), new_due_date (default today + 14 days, editable), open_cents_at_time, text snapshot, pdf_sha256/path, created_at). Creating a reminder makes no journal entry and changes no amounts (no fees, no interest).
32. `GET/POST /verkoop/{id}/herinnering`: GET shows a preview with editable date/new due date and the proposed Dutch text; POST stores the reminder, generates and stores the PDF, redirects to the invoice with a notice and download link. The text is a plain-Dutch template in domain code (level 1 friendly, 2 firm, 3 final; always names invoice number, date, original due date, open amount, new due date, IBAN) and shown in a copyable text box for pasting in an email, with a `mailto:` link prefilled when the klant has an email.
33. The invoice list/detail shows reminder history ("Herinnering 1 verstuurd op ...") and the debiteuren overview shows a marker for invoices with a reminder. There is no "verstuurd" tracking beyond creation (the owner sends it himself); the label says "Herinnering gemaakt".

### PDF generation
34. Library: ReportLab (platypus), pure Python wheel, no system libraries, works locally on macOS without Docker. Neither ReportLab nor WeasyPrint is installed in `.venv` today (checked); builder adds `reportlab` to `pyproject.toml` dependencies (`uv add reportlab`, commit `uv.lock`) and `pypdf` to the dev group for text extraction in tests. WeasyPrint was rejected: needs Pango/Cairo system libs (Homebrew) and is fragile on install.
35. `app/domain/sales/pdf.py` has pure functions `render_invoice_pdf(doc: InvoiceDocument) -> bytes` and `render_reminder_pdf(...)`, taking a plain dataclass snapshot (no DB, no Session). Uses built-in Helvetica (builder verifies the euro sign renders; otherwise register a TTF bundled under `app/web/static/fonts/`). Output is A4, multi-page safe (line table splits, totals not orphaned), deterministic enough to hash (set `invariant=1` on the canvas so repeated renders of the same data are byte-identical).
36. On finalize the PDF is generated from the frozen snapshot and stored content-addressed via the existing document storage helper (`app/domain/documents/storage.py`, under `data/documents/`), `pdf_sha256` and `pdf_path` saved on the invoice. Download `GET /verkoop/{id}/pdf` serves the stored file; for a concept it renders a watermark-free preview marked "CONCEPT" (not stored). PDF files of finalized invoices and reminders are never deleted (bewaarplicht). If PDF rendering fails at finalize, the whole finalize rolls back with the plain message "De PDF kon niet worden gemaakt, er is niets vastgelegd. Probeer het opnieuw." (file write happens before commit; an orphaned content-addressed file on rollback is harmless).

## 5. Technical approach

### Data model (Alembic `0012_sales_invoices.py`; models in `app/models/sales.py`, exported from `app/models/__init__.py`; migration must not import `app.models`)
- `contact.payment_term_days` INTEGER NULL (CHECK null or 0..365). `ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN`, add to `Contact` model, relation form/validation (small edit, builder B owns relations template/service touch).
- `sales_number_counter(year INTEGER PK, last_number INTEGER NOT NULL CHECK >= 0)`.
- `sales_invoice`: `id`; `kind` CHECK in ('invoice','credit_note'); `status` CHECK in ('concept','definitief'); `contact_id` FK NOT NULL; `credited_invoice_id` FK sales_invoice NULL (CHECK: only when kind='credit_note'); `number` TEXT NULL, `number_year` INT NULL, `number_seq` INT NULL (partial unique index on `number` where not null, unique on (number_year, number_seq)); `invoice_date` DATE NOT NULL; `service_date` DATE NULL; `payment_term_days` INT NOT NULL; `due_date` DATE NOT NULL; `due_date_manual` BOOL; `reference` TEXT NULL; `note` TEXT NULL; snapshot columns set at finalize `seller_name, seller_address_line, seller_postal_code, seller_city, seller_country, seller_kvk, seller_btw_id, seller_iban, seller_email, seller_phone, buyer_name, buyer_address_line, buyer_postal_code, buyer_city, buyer_country, buyer_btw_number, buyer_kvk` (all NULL while concept); `total_excl_cents, total_vat_cents, total_incl_cents` INT NOT NULL default 0 (CHECK >= 0; positive for credit notes too); `paid_cents` INT NOT NULL default 0 CHECK >= 0 (bank payments / refunds only); `journal_entry_id` FK UNIQUE NULL; `pdf_sha256`, `pdf_path` TEXT NULL; `finalized_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at` DATETIME. CHECK: status='definitief' implies number, journal_entry_id, pdf_sha256 and snapshot name not null. Indexes on `contact_id`, `invoice_date`, `status`.
- `sales_invoice_line`: `id, invoice_id FK, position INT, description TEXT, quantity_milli INT CHECK > 0, unit TEXT default 'stuk', unit_price_cents INT CHECK >= 0, vat_code FK vat_code, account_id FK account, line_excl_cents INT`. For credit notes `original_line_id` FK NULL.
- `sales_invoice_vat`: `invoice_id, vat_code, rate_bp, base_cents, vat_cents` (written at finalize), PK (invoice_id, vat_code).
- `sales_credit_application`: `id, credit_note_id FK, invoice_id FK, amount_cents CHECK > 0, created_at`. Triggers: no UPDATE/DELETE.
- `sales_reminder`: `id, invoice_id FK, level INT CHECK 1..3, reminder_date DATE, new_due_date DATE, open_cents INT, body_text TEXT, pdf_sha256, pdf_path, created_at`. Triggers: no UPDATE/DELETE.
- Triggers on `sales_invoice`/lines/vat as in AC 4. The UPDATE trigger on a definitief invoice must allow ONLY `paid_cents` and `updated_at` changes (a `WHEN` clause comparing all other columns with `IS`), and allow the concept to definitief transition (OLD.status='concept'). Builder verifies by test that finalize UPDATE works and an edit after finalize aborts.
- Migration test pattern as `tests/test_migrations.py`: upgrade/downgrade 0012, constraints, triggers.
- Migration `0013_bank_match_sales.py` (reconcile): rebuild `bank_match` with source CHECK extended to `('purchase_invoice','opening_item','sales_invoice')`, copying rows, recreating indexes and both triggers (take the SQL from 0011, not duplicated by hand if the helpers can be imported as constants; must not import `app.models`). `down_revision = "0012_sales_invoices"`.

### Domain (`app/domain/sales/`, no logic in routes; pure parts first)
- `types.py`: dataclasses `LineInput`, `VatGroup`, `Totals`, `InvoiceDocument` (frozen snapshot for PDF), `AgeingRow`, `ReminderDraft`.
- `amounts.py` (pure): `line_excl(quantity_milli, unit_price_cents)`, `compute_totals(lines, rates) -> Totals` (AC 10), `due_date(invoice_date, days)`, `parse_quantity`.
- `numbering.py`: `format_number(year, seq)`, `next_number(session, year)` (counter row upsert + increment inside the caller's locked transaction).
- `entries.py` (pure): `build_entry(invoice_snapshot, accounts: SalesAccounts{debtors, vat_high, vat_low}, journal_id) -> NewEntry` for both kinds; validated with `validate_entry`.
- `validation.py` (pure): `check_finalizable(...)` implementing AC 6/3/15, raising `SalesError(LedgerError)` with Dutch `message` (own module `errors.py`, codes e.g. `NOT_FINALIZABLE`, `MISSING_FIELD`, `DATE_ORDER`, `IMMUTABLE`, `UNSUPPORTED_VAT`, `OVER_CREDIT`).
- `service.py` (DB): `create_concept`, `save_concept` (header + lines replace), `delete_concept`, `finalize(session, invoice_id) -> SalesInvoice` (single transaction: lock, validate, snapshot, totals + vat rows, number, post_entry, set `journal_entry_id`, render and store PDF, status), `create_credit_concept(session, invoice_id)`, `list_documents(session, q, status, page)`, `get_detail`, `open_amount`, `derived_status(...)`, `debiteuren_overview(session, today) -> list[DebtorRow]` with ageing buckets, `stats(session, today)`, `suggest_defaults(session, contact_id)`.
- `reminders.py`: `eligible(...)`, `next_level(...)`, `draft_reminder(session, invoice_id, today)`, `create_reminder(session, invoice_id, ...)`, `reminder_text(level, ...)` (pure).

## 8. Risks and decisions made
- Decision: no email sending. No SMTP is configured or planned before the IMAP intake feature; sending from a local single-user app needs credentials and deliverability work that is not core to compliance. Replacement: PDF download, `mailto:` with prefilled subject/body, copyable text. A later "Verzenden" can reuse the stored PDF and reminder text. Not a blocking business question.
- Decision: ReportLab over WeasyPrint (no system libraries, pure wheel, deterministic output). Fixed layout in code instead of HTML templates.
- Decision: one shared gapless series for invoices and credit notes, per calendar year of the invoice date, with the ascending-date guard. Avoids two series to explain; consistent with Wet OB ("uniek volgnummer").
- Decision: VAT rounded once per rate group (allowed and common); PDF prints stored values so PDF, entry and later aangifte agree to the cent.
- Decision: finalized invoices are corrected only by credit notes. Ledger storno remains technically possible and is handled by derived status plus a warning, as on the purchase side; the number is never reused.
- Decision: `paid_cents` = bank payments only; credit-note settlement lives in its own append-only table so undo/recompute of bank matches cannot lose it.
- Decision: snapshot seller/buyer data at finalize; the stored PDF is the legal document and is kept (7-year retention).
- Decision: payment term default per klant (nullable column) else 30 days; no company-wide setting yet (YAGNI; can be added in Bedrijfsgegevens later).
- Decision: reminders have no fees/interest and no email tracking; they are letters the owner sends himself; levels only change wording.
- Decision: only 21/9/0% sales supported now; verlegd/EU/export/KOR refused with a plain message until the BTW-aangifte feature.
- Risk: the `bank_match` table rebuild (0013) must preserve data and triggers exactly; mitigated by migration test with existing rows. The reconcile package has crediteur assumptions (account `3000`, direction rules) threaded through several functions; C must keep all existing reconcile tests green.
- Risk: immutability trigger on `sales_invoice` must still allow `paid_cents` updates and the finalize transition; covered by trigger tests.
- Risk: `CompanyProfile.missing_for_invoice()` coverage vs art. 35a; A verifies and extends.
